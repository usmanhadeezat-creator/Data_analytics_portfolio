**Sales Dashboard Analysis**

**## Project Overview**
This project analyzes sale data to evaluate business performance, identify trends, and generate insights that support better business decisions.

**##Objectives**
-Analyze overall sales performance.
-identify top performing products.
-Evaluate sales across customer segments.
-examine sales trends over time.
-provide insights to support business decisions.

**##Tools Used**
-Microsoft excel 2019.
-pivotTables.
-PivortCharts.
-Slicers.
-Timeline.
-Excel formulas.

**##Analysis Performed**
-Data cleaning and preparation.
-Sales analysis.
-Product performance.
-Customer segment analysis.
-Dashboard creation.
